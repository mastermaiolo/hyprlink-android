HyprLink connects your Android device to your Linux workstation running Hyprland, delivering seamless, fast, and secure local network integration.

All communications run over the QUIC protocol with mutual TLS (mTLS) authentication using locally generated X.509 certificates and QR code pairing. No cloud servers, accounts, or proprietary services required.

Key Features:
- Bidirectional Audio: Stream PC audio to your phone and control media playback and volume directly on your screen.
- Virtual Webcam & Microphone: Use your phone's camera and microphone on your Linux PC with low latency.
- File Sharing & Auto-Sync: Seamlessly transfer photos, text, and screenshots organized automatically into dedicated folders on your PC.
- Remote Control & Shortcuts: Virtual touchpad, lock, suspend, and customizable Hyprland dispatchers accessible from Quick Settings Tiles.
- Find My Phone: Trigger a high-priority sound alert from your computer to locate your phone.
- Complete Privacy: Zero tracking, no telemetry, and 100% open-source communication.
